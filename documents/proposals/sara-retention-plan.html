The Opportunity

Why retention, and why now

Electronics retail has a structural problem: customers buy a TV, then disappear for four years. Every sale is won again from scratch against Amazon, Flipkart and Croma — usually on price. Retention is how Sara stops competing on price alone.

A repeat customer costs nothing to acquire, converts faster, and rarely asks for the lowest price. The goal is simple — give people a reason to come back that competitors cannot copy.

The three retention levers for an appliance retailer

LEVER 1

Stored value

Sara Coins that only spend at Sara. A balance you would forfeit by shopping elsewhere is the single strongest switching cost in retail.

LEVER 2

Useful contact

Warranty expiry, service due, filter replacement. Reasons to message a customer that they genuinely want — not discount spam.

LEVER 3

Human advantage

Installation, service and 40+ stores. A marketplace cannot send an engineer to your home or let you see the product first.

The strategic point: Sara cannot out-discount a marketplace, and should not try. It can out-serve them. Every strategy in this plan is built on service and relationship, not on cutting price.

The 12 Strategies · Part 1
Foundations & Quick Wins

Strategies 1–6

The first three are plumbing — unglamorous, but they unlock everything after them. Strategies 4 to 6 are the quick wins: low effort, fast payback, and independent of the loyalty programme.

1

Customer profile & history FOUNDATION

You cannot retain a customer you cannot see.
What
A single record per customer: total spend, order count, first and last purchase, categories bought, tier, Coin balance, contact preferences.
Why
Every other strategy reads from this. Without it there is no segmentation, no tiers, no personalisation.
Unlocks
Strategies 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12
2

Consent & preference centre FOUNDATION COMPLIANCE

Permission is the licence to operate every campaign below.
What
Explicit opt-in per channel (email / WhatsApp), one-click unsubscribe, and a customer-facing preference page.
Why
Required by India’s data protection law. Also protects sender reputation — without it, messages land in spam and the whole programme underperforms.
Guardrail
Frequency caps so no customer is over-contacted across campaigns.
3

Reliable automation scheduler FOUNDATION

Every reminder in this plan depends on jobs that actually run.
What
Move scheduled jobs to the hosting platform’s native scheduler, with secured trigger endpoints.
Why
The current approach silently fails in production — reminders would simply never send.
Unlocks
Strategies 4, 5, 7, 8, 9
4

Abandoned-cart recovery FASTEST ROI

Already written. Currently switched off. Pure recovered revenue.
What
Automatic reminders when a customer leaves items in their cart, over email and WhatsApp.
Why
These are people who already chose a product and got interrupted — the highest-converting audience in e-commerce. The engine exists; switching it on is the single quickest return in this plan.
Additions
WhatsApp version, suppression once purchased, and strict frequency caps so it never nags.
5

Back-in-stock & price-drop alerts QUICK WIN

Customers have already told us exactly what they want.
What
Notify customers when a wishlisted item returns to stock or drops in price.
Why
Wishlists are the strongest purchase-intent signal in the business and are currently unused. High conversion, low effort, and independent of every other strategy.
6

Review requests, rewarded

Reviews sell to new customers and pull existing ones back — a compounding loop.
What
A week after delivery, invite the customer to review their purchase and thank them with a discount coupon on their next order.
Why
Lifts conversion for future buyers, creates a second reason to return to the site, and surfaces service problems early.
Later
Once Sara Coins launches (Strategy 10), the coupon reward is swapped for Coins.

The 12 Strategies · Part 2
Lifecycle, Loyalty & Growth

Strategies 7–12

These build the long-term moat — the reasons a customer stays with Sara for a decade rather than a single transaction. Strategies 10 to 12 form the loyalty programme, delivered together.

7

Warranty & service reminder engine BIGGEST DIFFERENTIATOR

Nobody else tells you your refrigerator warranty expires next month.
What
Track purchase date and warranty period per appliance. Alert the customer before expiry, and at natural service intervals.
Why
Genuinely useful — customers welcome it. It creates a legitimate reason to contact them for years after the sale, drives service revenue, and positions Sara as a caretaker rather than a shop.
Edge
A marketplace cannot do this credibly. Sara’s engineers and stores can.
8

Replenishment & accessory journeys

Right message, right moment, based on what they actually bought.
What
Category-timed follow-ups — water purifier filters at six months, air-conditioner servicing before summer, accessories two weeks after delivery.
Why
Converts a single appliance sale into a recurring consumables relationship, shortening the four-year repurchase gap.
9

Customer segmentation & win-back

Stop treating a first-time buyer and a lapsed loyal customer the same way.
What
Group customers by how recently, how often and how much they buy — Champions, Loyal, At Risk, Lapsed, Lost — and message each differently.
Win-back
Lapsed customers receive a time-limited offer built for their category and past spend, rather than a generic discount blast.
Later
Once Coins exist, win-back becomes far stronger — “your Coins expire soon” outperforms any discount.
10

Sara Coins loyalty engine FLAGSHIP

The core retention mechanic — a balance customers lose by shopping elsewhere.
What
Real earn-and-redeem currency. Customers earn Coins on every completed purchase and spend them at checkout. Full statement visible in their account.
Design
Earn on delivery, not payment — so returns do not leak money. Redemption capped at a set share of order value to protect margin. Expire after 12 months of inactivity to create urgency and cap liability.
Reuses
The proven B2B partner wallet ledger — a tested blueprint, not a from-scratch build.
Why here
The largest build in the plan, and the anchor of Phase 3 — referral and tiers both run on it. Scheduled once the quick wins are banked and customer data is trustworthy.
11

Refer & earn programme

Referred customers are cheaper to acquire and stay longer.
What
Every customer gets a share code. Both sides earn Coins once the new customer’s first order is delivered.
Safeguards
Self-referral blocking, duplicate phone and device detection, minimum order value, reward only on delivery.
Depends on
Strategy 10
12

Membership tiers & gamified engagement

Status is a powerful, and free, retention tool.
What
Silver, Gold and Platinum tiers based on annual spend, each unlocking real benefits — free installation, extended returns, priority support, longer warranty cover.
Key nudge
“You are ₹X away from Gold” is consistently one of the strongest drivers of incremental spend in loyalty programmes.
Gamified
Tie the existing spin wheel to purchases and streaks, rather than a purely daily free spin.

Principles we will build to

Coins are treated as money

All balances and redemptions calculated on the server and tied to the logged-in customer. A customer can never influence their own balance from the browser.

Permission before promotion

No automated message without recorded consent and a working unsubscribe. Frequency caps applied across every campaign type.

Useful beats frequent

A warranty reminder earns goodwill. A third discount email in a week loses a customer permanently. Value density over volume.

Promise only what we deliver

Every benefit advertised must be operationally honoured in-store and online. No feature ships with unbacked marketing copy.

Deliberately out of scope

ExcludedReason

How we will know it is working. Repeat purchase rate and 90-day retention are the headline measures, reviewed monthly. Cart recovery rate reports first, within weeks of launch. Unsubscribe rate is the guardrail — if it climbs, we reduce frequency before adding campaigns.
