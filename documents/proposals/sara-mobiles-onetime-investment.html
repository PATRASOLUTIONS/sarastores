One-Time Investment Model
01 · The Commercial Model

Build it once. Own it outright.

This option replaces the revenue-share model with a fixed one-time development fee plus a transparent annual support plan. Sara pays a defined amount, the platform is delivered, and ownership transfers on final payment.

Why choose the one-time model

  • Full ownership of source code, database & platform on final payment.
  • No revenue share — 100% of online sales stay with Sara.
  • Predictable cost — one fixed bill, then a known annual support fee.
  • A real digital asset on Sara's books, not a rented service.

What to keep in mind

  • Sara funds the build upfront (vs. ₹0 in the revenue-share model).
  • Hosting (Azure) and support are billed separately as ongoing costs.
  • Bytewise carries no sales risk — so the incentive alignment differs from revenue-share.
  • New modules beyond agreed scope are handled as paid change requests.
Two ways to partner: Option A Revenue-share (₹0 upfront, 12–15% of sales — Bytewise runs it) · Option B One-time build (₹2–3 lakh, Sara owns it). This document covers Option B.
