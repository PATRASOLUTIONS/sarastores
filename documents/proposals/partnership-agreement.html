3. RECITALS

WHEREAS Bytewise is engaged in e-commerce platform development, hosting, and maintenance; and

WHEREAS Sara/Systech is engaged in retail and wholesale sale of mobile phones and electronics; and

WHEREAS the parties now wish to enter a joint venture revenue-sharing arrangement for the Platform;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of mutual covenants, the parties agree as follows:

CLAUSE 1 - DEFINITIONS

"Platform" means the e-commerce platform developed, hosted, and operated by Bytewise, including website, admin panel, APIs, and databases. "Revenue" means gross sales through the Platform, excluding cancelled/refunded/fraud orders, GST, and chargebacks. "Revenue Share" means the percentage payable by Sara to Bytewise per Schedule A. "Effective Date" means 03 August 2026.

CLAUSE 2 - SCOPE OF JOINT VENTURE

2.1 Bytewise Obligations

(a) Design, develop, deploy, and maintain the e-commerce Platform on Microsoft Azure;

(b) Provide hosting, CDN, SSL, database management, security, and disaster recovery;

(c) Keep the Platform as it exists on the Effective Date live, and provide bug fixes and security patches. New modules and enhancements are governed by Clause 2.3;

(d) Provide L1/L2 technical support (Mon-Fri, 10 AM - 6 PM IST);

(e) Perform DevOps, CI/CD, infrastructure management, and performance optimization;

(f) Provide SEO/GEO optimization, analytics, and reporting services;

(g) Manage product data, content updates, and catalogue maintenance up to 2,000 SKUs;

(h) Ensure platform security and compliance with applicable laws.

(i) Execute marketing campaigns, advertising, and promotional activities jointly with Sara, including spending ad budget on Google Ads, Meta Ads, and other digital advertising channels;

2.2 Sara Obligations

(a) Manage inventory, pricing, stock levels, and product procurement;

(b) Handle order dispatch, packaging, delivery, and logistics;

(c) Provide customer service including calls, returns, refunds, and warranty claims;

(d) Execute marketing campaigns, advertising, and promotional activities jointly with Bytewise;

(e) Provide brand assets, product data, pricing, and GST/bank details;

(f) Maintain pricing on a daily basis;

(g) Bear Azure Cloud Costs when monthly revenue falls below the Monthly Minimum Revenue Target.

2.3 In-Scope Work and Change Requests

In-scope services are: (i) hosting and operating the Platform as it exists on the Effective Date; (ii) bug fixes and security patches; (iii) catalogue maintenance up to 2,000 SKUs; and (iv) up to twenty (20) hours per calendar month of minor enhancements. New modules — including without limitation EMI, exchange/buyback, loyalty, native mobile applications, and any feature not live on the Effective Date — require a written change request and either a fixed fee or an additional revenue-share percentage, as agreed in writing. Hours above the monthly cap shall be billed at Rs. 2,500 (Rupees Two Thousand Five Hundred) per hour plus applicable GST, payable with the next monthly invoice. Unused hours do not roll over.

CLAUSE 3 - REVENUE SHARE AND PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 Revenue Share Structure

The applicable Revenue Share percentage, phase targets, and duration are set out in Schedule A. If actual revenue exceeds the current phase target, that phase's Revenue Share applies. If revenue falls below the current phase target but meets the preceding phase's target, the preceding phase's Revenue Share applies.

3.2 Payment Schedule

(a) Revenue Share is calculated monthly. By the 10th of each month, both parties reconcile the previous month's records. Sara shall provide sales data and payment gateway settlements for verification.

(b) By the 15th of each month, Sara shall deposit the confirmed Revenue Share into Bytewise's designated bank account via NEFT/RTGS/IMPS.

(c) Bytewise shall issue a monthly invoice on or before the 5th of each month detailing Net Revenue, applicable percentage, and amount due.

3.3 Late Payment and Suspension

If Sara fails to make any payment within 20 days after the monthly settlement date (i.e., by the 5th of the following month), interest shall accrue at 1.5% per month on the outstanding amount from the due date until actual payment. If any Revenue Share remains unpaid fifteen (15) days after the due date in Clause 3.2(b), Bytewise may, after written notice to Sara, suspend the storefront and pause Sara's licence to use the Platform until all outstanding amounts (including interest) are received in full. Customer and order data shall remain intact. Upon full payment, Bytewise shall restore access within two (2) business days. Bytewise shall have no liability for lost sales, lost profits, or other claims arising from such suspension.

3.4 Audit

Bytewise may, on not less than seven (7) days' written notice, once per calendar quarter, audit Sara's books, sales records, and payment-gateway settlement reports solely to verify Revenue and Revenue Share. Payment-gateway settlements shall be the source of truth where they conflict with Sara's internal records. If an audit shows understatement of more than five percent (5%) of the Revenue Share due for the audited period, Sara shall pay the shortfall, Bytewise's reasonable audit costs, and interest under Clause 3.3, within fifteen (15) days of the audit report.

CLAUSE 4 - PLATFORM HOSTING AND INFRASTRUCTURE

4.1 Hosting

The Platform shall be hosted on Microsoft Azure (App Service, Cosmos DB, CDN, AI Search, Blob Storage). Bytewise shall manage all Azure resources.

4.2 Uptime & Security

Bytewise shall maintain 99.5% monthly uptime (excl. scheduled maintenance). Maintenance shall occur 12 AM - 6 AM IST with 48 hours notice. Critical outages shall be resolved within 4 hours. Bytewise shall implement SSL, rate limiting, bot detection, security headers, and regular patching. Data breaches shall be notified within 24 hours.

4.3 Backups

Daily automated backups retained for 30 days, stored in a geographically separate Azure region.

CLAUSE 6 - CATALOGUE AND SKU MANAGEMENT

This Agreement covers up to 2,000 SKUs. Excess SKUs require written change request and additional fees. Bytewise shall provide one (1) dedicated Data Entry Operator (cost included in Development Costs). Sara shall provide accurate product data; Bytewise is not liable for errors from inaccurate data.

CLAUSE 7 - INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND DATA

(a) Bytewise retains ownership of Platform source code, technology, tools, and pre-existing intellectual property at all times. Sara has a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the Platform during the Term, only while invoices are current. Open-source and third-party components (including payment-gateway software) are not assigned.

(b) Customer and order data are jointly owned. Sara has full access via admin panel. Bytewise shall not use Sara's customer data for any other purpose.

(c) Upon termination or expiry, Bytewise shall, within 30 days, provide Sara an export of customer and order data. Platform source code, Platform documentation, and related technology shall be transferred only if Sara first pays the Source Code Buyout Fee, being the greater of (i) Rs. 15,00,000 (Rupees Fifteen Lakhs Only) or (ii) Clause 9.4 component (A) plus twelve (12) months of Revenue Share calculated on the trailing three-month monthly average actually received by Bytewise. If the Buyout Fee is paid, Bytewise shall complete the transfer and provide 30 days' transition support. Natural expiry does not waive the Buyout Fee. No source code is transferred for free.

CLAUSE 8 - CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other (business plans, financial data, customer info, technical specs, trade secrets). Neither party shall disclose to third parties without consent. Exceptions: publicly available info, pre-existing knowledge, independent development, or legal requirement. Obligations survive termination for 3 years.

CLAUSE 9 - TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 Term: 36 months from Effective Date. Auto-renews for 12-month periods unless 90 days' notice of non-renewal. Renewal Revenue Share: mutually agreed, or Phase 3 (1.5%) continues.

9.2 Termination for Convenience: 90 days' written notice by either party.

9.3 Termination for Cause: Immediate upon material breach (uncured after 30 days' notice), insolvency, bankruptcy, or non-payment for 30 days past due.

9.4 Kill Fee (convenience or Sara breach): The estimated cost of Platform development undertaken by Bytewise is Rs. 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs Only). If this Agreement is terminated by Systech/Sara for convenience at any time during the Term, or by Bytewise on account of Systech's/Sara's material breach, Systech/Sara shall pay a kill fee within thirty (30) days of termination equal to: (A) Unrecovered Amount = Rs. 5,00,000 minus Total Revenue Share actually received by Bytewise up to termination (not less than zero); plus (B) three (3) months of Revenue Share calculated on the trailing three-month monthly average actually received by Bytewise, provided that component (B) shall not be less than Rs. 1,00,000 (Rupees One Lakh Only). If fewer than three months of Revenue Share have been received, component (B) shall be Rs. 1,00,000. This Clause 9.4 shall not apply to (a) natural expiry of the initial Term or a renewal term, or (b) termination arising solely due to Bytewise's material breach. Natural expiry does not include a source-code transfer; Clause 7(c) continues to apply.

9.5 Effects of Termination: Sara shall pay all outstanding amounts within 15 days (including unpaid Revenue Share, kill fee under Clause 9.4, and any Source Code Buyout Fee if Sara elects a transfer under Clause 7(c)). Bytewise shall export Sara's customer and order data per Clause 7(c). Source code shall be transferred only if the Buyout Fee is paid. Each party shall return/destroy Confidential Information. Surviving clauses: 3.4 (Audit), 7 (IP), 8 (Confidentiality), 9.4 (Kill Fee), 10 (Liability), 11 (Disputes), 12 (General, including 12.8 and 12.9).

CLAUSE 10 - LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

(a) Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, lost-profits, or punitive damages. Bytewise's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement (including indemnity, contract, and tort), other than for Bytewise's fraud or willful misconduct, is capped at the total Revenue Share actually received by Bytewise in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. If no Revenue Share has been received in that period, the cap is zero. The cap applies to claims of IP infringement by the Platform. Sara's payment of Revenue Share, kill fee, Buyout Fee, and Sara's indemnity under (c) are not subject to this cap.

(b) Bytewise Indemnification: Subject to the cap in (a) (except for Bytewise's fraud or willful misconduct), Bytewise shall indemnify Sara against third-party claims arising from Bytewise's gross negligence or willful misconduct, IP infringement by the Platform (excluding Sara's content, product data, and third-party components), or data-protection breaches caused by Bytewise.

(c) Sara Indemnification: Sara shall fully indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Bytewise against all claims, losses, penalties, and costs (including legal fees) arising from: product liability; consumer-protection or e-commerce laws; GST, TDS, or other tax on sales; warranty and after-sales; dispatch, delivery, returns, and refunds; inaccurate, infringing, or unlicensed product data, images, descriptions, and catalogue content (including content obtained from Amazon or other third-party sources); and Sara's gross negligence or willful misconduct. Sara's indemnity is unlimited and is not reduced by Clause 10(a).

(d) Force Majeure: Neither party liable for failure/delay due to circumstances beyond reasonable control (natural disasters, war, epidemics, government action, telecom/power failure). Affected party shall promptly notify and mitigate.

CLAUSE 11 - DISPUTE RESOLUTION

(a) Disputes first resolved through good-faith negotiation within 30 days of written notice.

(b) If unresolved, referred to mediation in Bhubaneswar, Odisha.

(c) If unresolved within 60 days, finally resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, by a sole arbitrator. Seat: Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Language: English.

(d) Courts of Bhubaneswar, Odisha have exclusive jurisdiction over non-arbitral matters.
