The short answer: three campaign families, not 54 different delivery systems.

You have 54 ready-made campaign creatives grouped into lifecycle, festival and promotion. You choose whom to contact using 28 audience filters. The two outbound campaign channels are email and WhatsApp. Separately, three retention engines implement nine reminder types.

Setting up a template does not activate an automatic journey. Manual email campaigns, WhatsApp planning/sending and scheduled email reminders are separate paths. There is no demonstrated all-in-one multi-step, cross-channel drip builder that automatically runs every template in sequence.

These are code-defined capabilities, not counts of your live customers, stored campaigns or delivered messages. Five separate legacy email starter templates also exist, including a Custom Template; database-saved templates can add more. They are not included in the modern library count of 54. A fourth category name, transactional-adjacent, exists in the type definition but has no catalogue entries.

1. What kinds of campaigns can I create?

13 lifecycle templates

Change the customer's relationship with the store

Welcome first-time buyers, grow a second order, reward loyal/VIP customers, recover shopping intent and reactivate customers who stopped purchasing.

Lifecycle is a creative category here. Most of these templates are selected and sent manually; their names do not create event triggers.

28 festival / seasonal templates

Market around an occasion

Diwali, Dhanteras, regional festivals, national holidays, seasonal sales and year-end events each have a starting creative.

The occasion calendar helps plan a campaign; it does not automatically schedule the broadcast. Confirm moving festival dates each year.

13 promotion templates

Give the customer a reason to act now

Category upgrades, flash sales, bank offers, EMI, new arrivals, clearance, referrals, store visits, spin-and-win and Super Sunday.

A template advertises an offer; it does not create a coupon, bank discount, referral ledger or campaign prize inventory.

2. Which customers can I market to?

The application tags customers into overlapping groups. One customer can be a loyal buyer, Gold member and TV buyer at the same time. The picker chooses a segment; it is not a demonstrated visual AND/OR rules builder.

Lifecycle / RFM: 6

RFM means recency, frequency and monetary value. These are purchase-based classifications, not email-open or website-browsing scores.

  • Champions: recent, high frequency or high spend
  • Loyal: recent buyers with stronger purchase value
  • Promising: recent buyers with lower value so far
  • At risk: last purchase 91-180 days ago
  • Lapsed: last purchase 181-365 days ago
  • Lost: over 365 days or no last-order date in the RFM scorer

Spend tiers: 3

Based on qualifying spend in the profile's rolling 365-day window.

  • Platinum: INR 100,000 or more
  • Gold: INR 25,000 to below 100,000
  • Silver: below INR 25,000

Purchase behaviour: 6

  • New customers: first order within the last 30 days
  • One-time buyers: exactly one order
  • Repeat buyers: two or more orders
  • Frequent buyers: five or more orders
  • Big basket: average order value at least INR 50,000
  • High spenders: lifetime spend at least INR 10,000

New customer is not exclusive to one-time buyers: a customer can place multiple orders within their first 30 days.

Engagement and shopping intent: 6

  • All customers
  • Active: ordered within 30 days
  • Dormant: no order within 30 days
  • Registered, never bought
  • Abandoned cart: currently has a non-empty cart
  • Wishlist holders: currently has saved products

The manual abandoned-cart audience does not mean four hours have elapsed; the scheduled recovery engine has its own age rules.

Category affinity: 5

  • TV buyers
  • Refrigerator buyers
  • Washing-machine buyers
  • AC / cooling buyers
  • Kitchen-appliance buyers

Derived from purchase category/name signals, not a real-time machine-learning recommendation engine.

Channel consent: 2

  • Email opted in
  • WhatsApp opted in

An email address or phone number alone is not permission to send marketing. The customer must have the relevant stored opt-in.

Anonymous visitors are not an automatic contact list. Registered users and past buyers can appear in audience data, but order-only guest recipients currently default to no marketing consent. Imported Excel email addresses are accepted for email sending only when matched to an existing opted-in recipient. Group totals overlap and must not be added together as unique customer counts.

3. How can a message reach the customer?

Email / admin send flow implemented

Personalized HTML email through SMTP

Choose a built-in or saved template, edit the subject/body, select opted-in recipients and confirm the send. The sender personalizes the name, adds an unsubscribe link and reports sent/failed counts.

The sender uses batches of 50 with a 30-second pause between batches. Provider credentials, inbox delivery and serverless execution limits still need release verification.

WhatsApp / admin UI currently previews

AskEva approved-template messages

The admin's WhatsApp option builds a dry-run plan. A separate backend send path requires AskEva configuration, WHATSAPP_CAMPAIGNS_ENABLED=true and an explicit confirm: true request.

Keep requireConsent: true. The API's consent option is configurable, so do not treat default preview filtering as an immutable guarantee for custom integrations.

The final admin Send button still sends email. Selecting WhatsApp in the audience step does not turn that final button into a WhatsApp broadcast. Use the UI for its labelled dry-run plan; do not assume a finished WhatsApp sending wizard exists.

WhatsApp template readiness and batching limits

All 54 modern campaign entries have a WhatsApp counterpart, but only two counterpart names match the source's local approved-template list:

  • Appliance retention push: the_epic_full_moon_day
  • Super Sunday: sara_super_sunday_week4

The other 52 catalogue entries do not match that list. This is a source-code inventory, not a fresh provider approval check. Reconfirm the actual approved template, language, parameters and account access before sending.

The plan advertises batches of 40 with 20-second delays. The current live API executor loops over messages sequentially without applying those pauses. Do not rely on the preview's timing estimate as enforced throttling.

No equivalent SMS or push campaign sender was found in these marketing paths. A stored SMS consent flag, analytics tags or an advertising banner is not an implemented SMS/push broadcast channel. Email and WhatsApp are separate sends, not automatic fallback channels.

4. What happens after I set up a campaign?

Flow A / manual email campaign

Choose creative → choose customers → send → review results

  1. Choose templateOpen /admin/customer-centric. Pick lifecycle, festival, promotion or a saved email template.
  2. CustomizeEdit subject/body, destinations and any valid offer. Review the personalized preview.
  3. Choose recipientsSelect a segment and Email. Inspect the actual opted-in recipients and select the intended customers.
  4. Final preview + confirmCheck exact recipient count, creative and links. Sending cannot be recalled.
  5. Send + historySMTP runs in batches. Review processed, sent and failed counts in campaign history.

Backend detail: /api/admin/send-campaign uses explicit recipient IDs and consent. Its segment field is a history label, not an independent server-side audience filter. Do not call it with only a segment name and assume that limits the send. The admin recipient picker loads up to 500 entries; check the selected list, not just the segment's total.

Flow B / WhatsApp plan and separately enabled backend send
  1. Select campaign + audienceChoose a modern library template, WhatsApp segment and intended recipient IDs.
  2. Preview onlyThe admin asks the API for a plan without confirm. Nothing is sent.
  3. Resolve readinessCheck opt-in, valid Indian phone numbers, duplicates, provider approval and configuration.
  4. Explicit backend confirmationA properly configured, authorized integration can request the live send. This is not the UI's email Send button.
  5. Interpret acknowledgementsThe response counts successful provider requests and failures; it does not prove the customer read the message.
Flow C / scheduled email retention
  1. Customer signalAn owned cart becomes stale, a wishlist product changes, or a purchase reminder becomes due.
  2. Scheduled scanThe corresponding guarded cron route runs. The engine checks its own eligibility rules.
  3. Consent + suppressionCheck the profile's email opt-in, address and applicable caps/history/frequency rules.
  4. Email reminderSend the engine's reminder creative with an unsubscribe link. These are not automatically the admin's edited template.
  5. Record outcomeStore reminder state/events or snapshots. Review failures and the retention overview.

No guaranteed inbox-to-purchase attribution. Campaign history exposes email send counts; retention exposes reminder counts. Provider success is not the same as delivery, opening, clicking or buying. Do not report conversions, revenue attribution or an automatic email-to-WhatsApp fallback without separately implementing and validating them.

5. The nine implemented reminder types

These are eligibility rules, not promises that each customer receives all nine messages. They run through three email engines: cart recovery, wishlist alerts and lifecycle reminders.

Code-defined reminder rules and customer destinations
Type / engineWhen the customer becomes eligibleIntent and limits
1. Abandoned cart
Cart recovery		 A signed-in customer's non-empty cart has been inactive for more than 4 hours, and is no older than 14 days. Bring them back to the cart. At most two reminders per stored cart reminder state. The second waits at least 44 hours after the first actual send.
2. Wishlist price drop
Wishlist alerts		 A saved product's price drops at least 5% compared with the previous snapshot. Link to that product. The first run establishes a baseline and sends no transition alert for an item without a previous snapshot.
3. Wishlist restock
Wishlist alerts		 A saved product changes from out of stock to in stock between snapshots. Invite the customer to buy. One wishlist alert per customer per run; a price drop takes priority when both changes apply to the selected item.
4. Review request
Lifecycle		 7-13 days after recorded delivery; older records without delivery dates fall back to purchase date. Ask for feedback. A qualifying order, available customer email, consent and lifecycle suppression checks are required.
5. Accessory cross-sell
Lifecycle		 14-20 days after purchase. Suggest complementary accessories. This is a timed reminder, not proof of personalized item-level recommendations or an automatically created offer.
6. Warranty notice
Lifecycle		 Within the last 30 days before the category-derived warranty expiry. Invite a cover/service discussion. The model defaults to 12 months; selected categories use 24. These estimates are not verified manufacturer warranty data.
7. Service due
Lifecycle		 Recurring category intervals: AC/purifier 182 days; washing machine/water heater 365 days, with a seven-day eligibility window per cycle. Encourage servicing only for mapped categories. The reminder does not establish that an appointment has been booked.
8. Consumable replenishment
Lifecycle		 Water-purifier cartridge or AC service/filter-clean interval of 182 days, with a seven-day eligibility window. Prompt replenishment or maintenance, subject to the same lifecycle frequency and eligibility checks.
9. Win-back
Lifecycle		 The profile is in At risk or Lapsed. The reminder key uses a 30-day time bucket. Invite a return purchase. Lost is available for manual campaigns, but is not selected by this automatic win-back engine.

Lifecycle frequency is deliberately selective

At most one eligible reminder per customer per run. Customers with a recent recorded reminder are suppressed for 14 days; duplicate keys are checked over a 60-day history window. A run is capped at 100 sends.

A review sent on day 7 can suppress the day 14-20 accessory window. Limited windows and competing rules mean reminders can be skipped, not automatically queued for later. These are not global caps across every manual campaign/channel.

Eligibility time is not the delivery time

The cron configuration is daily. A four-hour-old cart is only processed on the next scheduled scan; this is not an exact four-hour timer. A delayed first send also moves the earliest second send.

Cart recovery scans at most 200 candidate carts per invocation; wishlist alerts cap sends at 100. Bounds protect execution time but do not prove every eligible customer will be processed on each run.

6. Example: how you can market to one customer

Imagine Meera, an illustrative customer. This is a planning example, not a live customer record or a claim that these touches run as one automatic sequence.

  1. She registers and opts in, but has not purchased

    Manual email
    Choose no_purchase and the Registered, never bought template. Use an actual configured first-order offer, not an invented coupon from the creative.

  2. She saves a television but is not ready to buy

    Scheduled wishlist email, if activated
    After a baseline exists, a price fall of at least 5% or a restock can trigger an alert. Alternatively, run a manual wishlist campaign after reviewing its claims.

  3. She adds it to her cart and leaves

    Scheduled cart-recovery email, if activated
    At the next scan, check inactivity, consent and cap. Send a cart reminder; if still eligible, a second touch waits at least 44 hours after the first. Do not continue sending indefinitely.

  4. She buys

    Manual welcome + selected lifecycle reminders
    She qualifies for recent/new-buyer targeting. You can manually send Welcome. Review and accessory reminders have their separate timing and suppression rules; do not promise both. Purchase confirmation itself is a transactional message, not this marketing template.

  5. A relevant seasonal offer arrives

    Manual campaign
    Select TV buyers for an upgrade campaign, or an appropriate festival audience. Choose email, or preview an approved WhatsApp counterpart. There is no automatic fallback if the email fails.

  6. She purchases again or reaches a higher spend tier

    Manual retention / reward campaign
    Target Repeat, Loyal, Gold or Platinum as applicable. Send a thank-you or VIP creative only when its advertised benefits actually exist. A tier-upgrade template is not an automatic trigger.

  7. She stops buying, or withdraws consent

    Win-back or stop
    At risk/Lapsed can become eligible for the email win-back engine. Lost can be targeted manually if appropriate. If she opts out of a channel, do not keep marketing to her on that channel.

The useful strategy is relevance, not maximum message volume. Select the next appropriate touch for the customer's state. Avoid combining a manual broadcast, cart reminder and WhatsApp push simply because all three paths exist.

7. What must be configured before this actually runs?

  1. Accurate customer data and consent. Profiles, order history, segments and channel opt-ins must be populated and current. Registration or a guest purchase is not automatic marketing permission.
  2. Valid creative, offer and destination. Verify discount eligibility, dates, stock, benefits and site origin. Checkout remains authoritative for coupon and pricing rules. An edited admin email is not automatically the retention engine's message.
  3. Working email provider. Configure the application's SMTP host, port, user, password and sender. Test authenticated delivery, unsubscribe and error handling with a controlled audience before any broad send.
  4. Working WhatsApp provider when used. Configure ASKEVA_API_KEY and ASKEVA_API_URL, appropriate sender, current approved templates and explicit send enablement. The existing UI is a preview, not a complete WhatsApp live-send wizard.
  5. Deployed and authenticated scheduling. Source now contains Vercel cron definitions. Confirm that the deployed plan supports them, set the matching CRON_SECRET of at least 16 characters, and verify actual scheduled execution. No hosted jobs were triggered for this guide.
  6. Preview the sending engines safely. Cart, wishlist and lifecycle routes support ?dryRun=1. Profile rebuild and segmentation are separate data-changing jobs, not message previews. A dry-run does not certify provider delivery or production throughput.
  7. Observe outcomes. Inspect failures, consent reach, truncation and reminder counts. SMTP/API acceptance is not proof of inbox delivery or read status. Establish measurement and operational ownership before scaling campaigns.
