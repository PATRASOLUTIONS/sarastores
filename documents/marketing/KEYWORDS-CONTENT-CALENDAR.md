Place the primary phrase naturally in the page title, H1 and introduction where accurate. Use descriptive image alt text and concise URLs. Meta descriptions should explain a real reason to choose the product/store. Do not add a `meta keywords` tag or repeat city names across hundreds of near-identical pages.

## Architecture and internal links

Preserve the existing `/product/[slug]`, category, brand, store, article, `/offers` and `/diwali-gift` routes. Link homepage → useful category/brand hubs → exact product pages; product pages → relevant buying guides, genuine related products and policy/terms pages. Store pages link only to real local services and relevant catalogue entry points.

An inactive campaign must stop advertising its gift. Retain a useful campaign explanation/archive when appropriate, clearly marked ended; choose redirects/404/410 based on actual long-term page purpose and preserve valuable incoming links. Do not create a new thin page every day or change product canonicals when offers change.
